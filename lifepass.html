Frequently asked

Can I track more than one device?

Yes — membership covers unlimited devices under one registry, each with its own ID and password.

Do mechanics need a Lifepass account?

No. A device's ID and its own password are enough to pull up its record — nothing about your shop account is exposed.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, membership is month to month with no lock-in.

Is my account password shared with devices?

Never — every device you register gets a separate password from the moment it's created.

ABOUT

Built for the shop floor, not the boardroom

Every device gets its own identity — a unique ID and its own password — so it can be found, and handed to a mechanic, without ever touching the shop's main account.
